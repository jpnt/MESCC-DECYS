# AI-use disclosure

We used AI assistants on this assignment, as permitted, and disclose that use here.

## Tools

- Claude Opus (Anthropic)
- Claude Sonnet (Anthropic)

## What they were used for

1. **Documentation writing.** Drafting and polishing the `README.org` writeups,
   using a draft -> critique -> revise workflow: the AI produced a draft, we
   critiqued it against the assignment rubric, and revised. All answers,
   offsets, hashes and secrets were produced from our own board-9 artifacts; the
   AI was used for wording and organization, not to invent results.

2. **Debugging cryptic parts.** Help understanding and fixing the more opaque
   steps of the project, mainly the `openssl` work (PEM unwrapping, raw RSA
   decryption of the signature, and reading the ASN.1/DER structure).

The artifacts and their derivation are our team's own work on our own assigned
device.
