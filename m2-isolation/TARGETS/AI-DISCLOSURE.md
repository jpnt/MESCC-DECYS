# AI-use disclosure

We used AI assistants on this assignment (M2 - Isolation), as permitted, and
disclose that use here.

## Tools

- Claude Opus (Anthropic)
- Claude Sonnet (Anthropic)

## What they were used for

1. **Documentation writing.** Drafting and polishing the `README.org` writeups
   for Parts 1-3, using a draft -> critique -> revise workflow: the AI produced a
   draft, we critiqued it against the assignment rubric, and revised. All
   capability sets, cap bitmasks, password hashes, layer SHA-256 values and
   command outputs were produced from our own lab runs on our assigned VM; the AI
   was used for wording and organization, not to invent results.

2. **Running and debugging the lab.** Help driving the Docker exercises over SSH
   on our assigned VM (`decys`) and understanding the more opaque parts -
   Mainly Part 3 container-escape work.
