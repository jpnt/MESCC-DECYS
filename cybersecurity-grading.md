- course main areas
    - security foundations (theat modelig, crypto)
    - system security (embeeded, iot)
    - web security (models, common attack vectors)
    - emerging  tech (AI, VR/AR)

- grading
    - research project (m4)
        - 35%
        - thru the semester
        - select one of four:
            - "Security Analysis of Existing Systems"
            - "Literature Survey or Systematization of Knowledge (SoK)"
            - "Development of Novel Security Solutions"
            - "Empirical Evaluation of Security Scenarios"
        - presentation/defense at the end of semester
        - deliverables:
            - initial idea presentation: week 4
            - checkpoint: week 8
            - progress report: week 10
            - final report and presentation: week 11
    - threat modeling report (m3)
        - 15%
        - week 1 to 5
        - based on owasp threat modeling
            - phases:
                - know and decompose application
                    - external deps
                    - entry points
                    - exit points
                    - assets
                    - trust levels
                    - data flow diagrams
                - determine and rank threats
                - determine countermeasures and mitigation
        - 2 lab classes
        - good resource: Threat Modeling: Designing for Security, Book by Adam Shostack
    - short projects (m1 and m2)
        - 50%
        - week 3 to 9
            - m1: 3-7: embedded/IoT
            - m2: 7-9: software isolation
        - submit writeup soon
